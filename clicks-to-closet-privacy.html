Clicks to Closet Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Clicks to Closet lets you sign in with Pinterest so we can show the pins you have already saved, sorted into shopping categories like tops, bottoms and shoes. This page explains what we access and what we do with it.

What we access from Pinterest

When you sign in, Pinterest asks you to approve read-only access to your boards and pins. We can then see the titles, descriptions, images and links of the pins on your public boards. We do not see your password, and we cannot post, save, edit or delete anything on your account.

How we use it

We use your pins only to display them to you on our site, sorted into categories. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything else.

What we store

  • A sign-in token from Pinterest, kept on our server so you stay signed in for up to 30 days, or until you choose Sign out of Pinterest.
  • A temporary copy of your pin list, kept for about 10 minutes so the site loads faster.

We do not keep a permanent database of your pins. Signing out, or restarting our server, removes the stored token and the temporary copy.

Your email address

The 20% off box on our homepage does not send or save your email address at this time. If that changes, we will update this page before collecting anything.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time with the Sign out of Pinterest button on our site. You can also remove our access at any time from your Pinterest account settings.
